Qibla Go

Face the Kaaba, wherever you are

A free Qibla direction finder with no ads and no account. It shows the direction to the Kaaba in Mecca on a map from any place on Earth, with the sun and the moon to help you orient yourself. The phone app adds a live compass that uses the phone's own sensors.

The bearing is the great-circle direction, computed on your device from your coordinates. Your location never leaves your device.
